Meet CampusMate AI 🌿

CampusMate brings your assignments, deadlines, and study files together in one simple space—so you can focus on learning, one step at a time.

🎓

What is CampusMate?

It is a personal college organizer designed to help you keep track of coursework without juggling scattered lists and files. Sign in to see your own assignments and study materials.

💚

Why was it made?

College gets busy, and it is easy to lose sight of what is due next. CampusMate makes the important things easier to see, encourages steady progress, and reminds you to take things one task at a time.

🧭

How do I use it?

  • Add an assignment with its title and deadline; attach a PDF or choose one from your gallery.
  • Use the All, Overdue, Completed, and Upcoming filters to find what needs your attention.
  • Mark work complete with its checkbox, or edit and delete assignments as your plans change.
  • Keep PDFs and images in your device-local gallery and open or remove them whenever you need.
🔒

Your account and files

Sign-in and assignment data use Firebase. PDFs and images are saved only in this browser's local storage and organized by your signed-in account. Files do not sync to another phone or laptop; clearing browser data can remove them. Study Q&A searches PDF text, assignments, and this page locally without sending your files or questions to an AI service.

🔔

Gentle deadline reminders

You can enable browser notifications for assignments due today. Notifications require your permission and a secure site (HTTPS or localhost); keep the dashboard open to receive reminders.

🌱

Made for progress, not pressure

CampusMate is here to help you plan, not to judge your pace. Break big workloads into manageable steps, leave room for rest, and celebrate what you finish.

Start wherever you are. Add one task, make a small plan, and take your next step.
